What we inspect

Where we put a nose to work.

Winnie is trained to trace hidden mold to its source – not just in houses, but anywhere moisture hides. Here's what we inspect and how each one works.

Residential

Homes

Single-family homes, condos, townhomes, and rentals. Winnie sweeps living spaces, basements, crawlspaces, and problem areas so you know whether mold is present – and where – before you buy, sell, or remediate.

  • Whole-home canine sweep, handler-read in real time
  • Basements, crawlspaces, and unfinished areas included
  • Pre-purchase and post-water-damage peace of mind
  • Confirmed with moisture meter and hygrometer readings, plus six included lab samples
Commercial

Commercial spaces

Businesses, offices, retail, and other commercial buildings. Non-invasive detection keeps your space open while we locate hidden moisture problems that affect air quality, tenants, and liability.

  • Offices, retail, and multi-tenant buildings
  • Non-invasive – no shutdown, no demolition to look
  • Written report suitable for owners, tenants, and insurers
  • Referrals to vetted hygienists and remediators
On wheels & water

Boats & RVs

Moisture gets trapped in boats and RVs just like buildings – and it's easy to miss. Winnie inspects the cabins, storage areas, and tight spaces where mold hides.

Boats RVs
  • Cabins, upholstery, and enclosed storage areas
  • Common after leaks, flooding, or long storage
  • Source-first detection before you tear anything apart
  • Priced individually – quoted before anything is booked
After remediation

Post-Remediation Verification

Remediation is done – but is it actually clear? Post-Remediation Verification (PRV) uses Winnie to confirm the source has been resolved, so you're not taking anyone's word for it.

  • Independent check after remediation work is complete
  • Confirms the source – not just the visible surface
  • Optional air or swab sampling on request
  • Documentation for your records and next steps
