2.Description of Services

Company provides certified canine mold detection inspection services using trained detection dogs and certified handlers, for residential and commercial properties. Services may include:

  • An on-site inspection (typical duration 60–90 minutes) consisting of a handler-led visual and moisture assessment of the Property using a moisture meter and hygrometer, followed by two (2) canine detection sweeps
  • Six (6) laboratory samples (air, swab, or bio-tape lift) collected at points of canine alert or handler discretion, included in the quoted inspection price at no additional per-sample charge
  • A same-day verbal walkthrough of findings on site
  • A written pre-final report delivered within approximately 24–48 hours, followed by a final written report including laboratory results and recommendations
  • A post-assessment review call of approximately fifteen (15) minutes following delivery of the final report
  • Referral and coordination support with independent certified industrial hygienists
  • Referral to independent, qualified remediation professionals
  • Laboratory samples in excess of the six (6) included, offered as an optional add-on service at Company’s then-current per-sample rate, disclosed to Client before any additional sample is collected

Company does not perform mold remediation, mycotoxin testing, or structural repair services of any kind. This separation is intentional and exists to eliminate conflicts of interest between detection findings and remediation recommendations. Company’s compensation for an inspection is a flat quoted fee that does not vary with the number, size, or severity of any condition identified. Referral compensation from third parties is addressed in Section 9.
