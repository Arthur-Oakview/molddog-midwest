Why it gets missed

You deserve solid proof of what's really in your home.

Air samples capture one spot at one moment. An ERMI is a composite dust sample built to represent the whole home – useful, but it can only tell you that mold DNA is present, never where it is coming from. Neither one points you at the source, so mold behind a wall, under flooring, or inside your HVAC keeps growing while the paperwork says you are fine. A canine assessment pinpoints the source producing the spores your dust test picked up.

How we work

How we find what others miss.

01

Scent first discovery

Mold releases microbial gases (MVOCs) as it grows, and leaves a scent profile long after. Winnie detects those gases at trace concentrations and follows them to the source – behind a wall, under flooring, or inside the HVAC.

Read more ▼
02

Proof, not a hunch

An alert alone won’t get you remediated. Following NORMI standards, we take six samples (air, swab, or bio-tape lift) across the HVAC-serviced areas of your home – included in your price. Most mold dog companies don’t sample at all.

Read more ▼
03

We read the "alert" and the building

A dog tells you mold is there. You need to know why. Using moisture meter and hygrometer readings alongside a trained visual inspection, we trace the alert back to its cause – an appliance installed wrong, a construction failure, a moisture path that never stopped.

Read more ▼
04

Non-invasive, start to finish

No drilling, no demolition, no exploratory holes. Disturbing mold to go looking for it spreads it. Winnie narrows the search first so your space stays intact.

Read more ▼
05

We don't disappear after the report

Finding mold is step one. We connect you to remediators we’ve personally vetted – never more than three per service area – and stay available while you work through it. Some of them pay us a small referral fee (under 5%); we don’t perform remediation ourselves, so there’s nothing for us to sell you by finding more mold than is there.
