How we work

Five foundational principles

01

Scent first discovery

Mold releases microbial gases (MVOCs) as it grows and has a scent profile long after. Winnie is trained to detect those gases at trace concentrations and trace them to the source – even when there's no visible sign on a reachable surface, like behind a wall, under flooring, or inside the HVAC.

Read more ▼
02

Proof, not a hunch

You need objective evidence to proceed in any case of needed remediation. As per NORMI professional standards, a minimum of 6 samples (air, swab, bio-tape lift) must be taken in all HVAC serviced areas of a structure to determine the extent of mold contamination. This is the objective evidence that most mold dog companies do not provide when your health is on the line.

Read more ▼
03

We read the "alert" and the building

A certified mold dog alerts on the presence of mold -- you need to know the root cause. As part of our investigation we utilize various tools and techniques to identify appliance installation failures, construction failures, and original sources of persistent moisture.

Read more ▼
04

Non-invasive, start to finish

A key benefit to a certified mold dog finding source mold is to to avoid any drilling or demolition that may disturb mold and further contaminate your space. We avoid this at all costs to keep your space intact.

Read more ▼
05

We don't disappear after the report

Finding mold is step one. We want to help you get connected to remediators we have personally vetted in your area -- only the best with the highest certifications and ability to clearly communicate their compliant techniques are recommended to you - never more than 3 in a service area.
