The process & our method
How accurate is a detection dog, really?
Trained detection dogs run far higher than conventional methods, and we back every alert with evidence. Rather than leaning on a single accuracy figure, we confirm what Winnie finds with a trained visual inspection, moisture meter and hygrometer readings, and six lab samples (air, swab, or bio-tape lift) taken at the alert points – so you walk away with objective evidence you can act on, not just a dog’s opinion.
What actually happens during an inspection?
I first walk the entire home without Winnie, assessing visually and with a moisture meter and hygrometer. Then I bring Winnie through the whole property, marking every area where she alerts. We make a second pass looking for consistent indications or new areas of interest that may have been missed, and take six samples at the alert points – included in your price, never billed per sample. Lastly, I walk you through everything we found and give you a recommendation for next steps.
What makes you different from other mold-dog companies?
Most can read their dog. Few can read the building. I'm trained in building science, so I can explain why the mold is there, account for spots a dog might miss, and sharpen how I read Winnie's cues. Just as important: I've lived this myself. That experience gives me real empathy for what you're going through and a persistence of discovery – I don't stop until we have a real answer.
Can you tell if the mold is old or active?
Often, yes. Active growth, dormant-but-viable, and old remediated damage look different – in staining, texture, moisture readings, and how the dog alerts. We read those signals together to tell you whether you're dealing with a current problem or a historical one, because that impacts what your immediate options are. That said, all mold – whether old or new – is considered a health concern, despite the varied opinions out there.
I already ran an ERMI. Why would I need a dog?
Because they answer different questions. An ERMI is a composite dust sample designed to represent the whole home, and it’s good at what it does – it tells you which mold DNA is present in your living space. What it cannot do is tell you where that mold is coming from. There is no source location in an ERMI score. The same is true of HERTSMI-2.

That’s the gap a canine assessment closes. Winnie traces the scent of active mold to the structure it is growing in – behind a wall, under flooring, inside the HVAC – so the spores your dust test captured have an address. Bring your ERMI to the consult; it’s genuinely useful context for where to work first.
Can you work with my functional medicine provider?
Yes, and we’d encourage it. A large share of our clients are already in care with a functional medicine, integrative, or environmental health provider and came to us because their practitioner suspected the home. Your final report is written to be shared – alert locations, moisture findings, photographs, and lab results in one document – and the 15-minute post-assessment call is open to your provider if you want them on it. We won’t interpret your labs or your symptoms; that is their job. Our job is to hand you both a clear, objective picture of the building.
What tools do you use besides the dog?
A moisture meter and a hygrometer are the two I rely on most, alongside a trained visual inspection of the structure. The meter tells me whether material is actively wet and how far the moisture travels; the hygrometer tells me whether the air in a space is holding conditions that let mold keep growing. Together with Winnie’s alerts and six lab samples, that is what turns “the dog alerted here” into “here is the source, here is why it is happening, and here is what it will take to fix it.”
Is it invasive? Will you cut into my walls?
No. The inspection is completely non-invasive – no drilling, no demolition. The dog finds the source first, so any future work is targeted instead of exploratory.
How do I prepare my home?
Keep the areas you want inspected accessible, secure other pets during the visit, and hold off on heavy cleaning or air fresheners beforehand so they don't interfere.
How soon do I get results?
A same-day verbal walkthrough on site, plus a written report with photos within 48 hours.
What if Winnie doesn't find anything?
That's a real, valuable result – ruling mold out with confidence is part of what you're paying for. You still get the walkthrough and report.
After the inspection
You don't do remediation – so what happens after you find mold?
We connect you with vetted remediators who meet our standards – never more than three in a service area – and make sure you understand your report before those conversations.Some of those remediators pay us a referral fee, always under 5% of their service charge. It’s worth saying plainly: we don’t perform remediation, and our inspection fee is flat regardless of what we find, so there’s nothing for us to gain by finding more mold than is there.
Do you offer a re-inspection after remediation? Do you offer post remediation verification testing?
We generally recommend an immediate re-inspection after remediation is performed to ensure the space was properly remediated and cleaned. We work with vetted 3rd party hygienists to ensure you are getting an unbiased assessment of the results of remediation. We also recommend annual inspections by a certified mold dog to ensure no new issues have come up or after any event that would make you question the health of your space.
Health & safety
Is the inspection safe for my kids and pets?
Yes. It's non-invasive and Winnie is a trained, focused working dog. We'll just have you secure other pets so she can work.
I'm sick and no one can tell me why – can you help?
Maybe. I’ve been there. We can’t diagnose you, but we can tell you whether hidden mold is in your environment and exactly where it is coming from – which is often the missing piece other inspections and tests overlook. Most of our clients are already working with a functional medicine or environmental health provider, and what you learn here is the piece that lets the two of you act.
